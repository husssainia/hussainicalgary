Who We Are

Rooted in faith.
Built around community.

Established in 1982, Hussaini Association of Calgary serves the Shia Muslim community in Calgary by providing religious, educational, cultural and community opportunities grounded in Fiqh-e-Jaffaria and the teachings of the Ahlul Bayt.

HAC seeks to nurture a vibrant community while encouraging fellowship, understanding, mutual respect, peace and harmony within Calgary's diverse society.
