Our Community

A Hussainia rooted in faith & community.

Hussaini Association of Calgary is a home for Calgary's Shia Muslim Community — a place where families, youth and community members come together for worship, learning, remembrance and service.

Our community is grounded in the teachings of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ), Imam Ali (ع), Imam Hussain (ع) and the Ahlul Bayt (ع), following the principles of Fiqh-e-Jafaria.
