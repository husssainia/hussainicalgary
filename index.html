About Us

Faith, community, education and service.

Hussaini Association of Calgary

Serving Calgary Since 1982

Hussaini Association of Calgary was established to serve the religious, educational and cultural interests of the Shia Muslim community in Calgary.

The Association provides resources, facilities and opportunities that help community members practice Islam and learn from the teachings of Prophet Muhammad and the Ahlul Bayt.

We also believe in fellowship, understanding, mutual respect and harmony with the broader community.
