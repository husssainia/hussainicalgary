Who We Are
More than a place of worship. A home for community.

Established in 1982, Hussaini Association of Calgary serves the Shia Ithna Ashari Muslim community in Calgary. Our community provides opportunities to practice Islam, learn, gather, volunteer and build meaningful relationships.

The legacy of Imam Ali (ع) continues to inspire our community: to seek knowledge, stand for justice, care for the vulnerable, and serve humanity with dignity.

The Spirit of Karbala
