Who We Are

More than a place.
A community.

Hussaini Association of Calgary is a Shia Ithna Ashari Muslim community organization established in 1982. Our mission is to provide resources, facilities and opportunities for the community to practice Islam and promote its teachings according to Fiqh-e-Jaffaria.
