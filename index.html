Who We Are

A community
rooted in faith.

Established in 1982, Hussaini Association of Calgary serves the Shia Muslim community in Calgary. HAC provides resources, facilities and opportunities that empower its members to practice Islam and promote its teachings according to Fiqh-e-Jaffaria.

Hussaini School

Growing
minds.
Building values.

Hussaini School helps children learn Quran, Fiqh, Aqaid, Akhlaq and Islamic History, with an optional Urdu class. The school focuses on preserving the true teachings of Islam and building strong values in the next generation.
