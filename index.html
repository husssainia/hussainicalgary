Who We Are

A community built around faith, family and service.

Hussaini Association of Calgary is a Shia Ithna Ashari Muslim community organization serving Calgary since 1982. Our mission is to provide a welcoming environment where families and individuals can practice Islam, learn, connect and contribute to the wider community.
