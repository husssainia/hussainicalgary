Who We Are

A home for
the community.

The Hussaini Association of Calgary was established in 1982 to serve the Shia Muslim community in Calgary. Our purpose is to provide resources, facilities and opportunities that allow our community to practice Islam and live by the teachings of Fiqh-e-Jaffaria.
