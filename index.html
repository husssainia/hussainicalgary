About HAC

A community rooted in faith & service.

Hussaini Association of Calgary is a registered charitable organization serving the Shia Muslim Community of Calgary.

Since 1982, HAC has provided a place where community members can gather, worship, learn, support one another and participate in programs based on Islamic teachings and Fiqh-e-Jafaria.
