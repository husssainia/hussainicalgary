Our Community

A home for faith, family and community.

Hussaini Association of Calgary is a Shia Muslim community organization serving Calgary since 1982.

Our community provides a place for worship, religious education, gatherings, youth activities, cultural programs and opportunities to serve others.

Rooted in the teachings of the Ahlul Bayt (ع), we strive to build a stronger and more connected community for generations to come.
