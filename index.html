Who We Are

Rooted in
faith.
Built for community.

Hussaini Association of Calgary was established in 1982 to serve the Shia Muslim community in Calgary. HAC provides resources, facilities and opportunities that empower members to practice Islam according to Fiqh-e-Jaffaria.

Our community is committed to religious learning, cultural enrichment, fellowship, mutual respect, community engagement and service.
