Who We Are

A community built on faith and service.

Hussaini Association of Calgary is a registered charitable organization established in 1982 to serve the Shia Muslim community in Calgary.

Our purpose is to provide resources, facilities and opportunities that empower our community to practice Islam and promote its teachings according to Fiqh-e-Jaffaria.

We strive to build a welcoming community centered around faith, education, fellowship, service and mutual respect.
