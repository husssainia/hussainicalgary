Who We Are

More than a place. A community.

A welcoming space for worship, learning, gathering and meaningful community life.

Hussaini Association of Calgary is a registered charitable organization serving the Shia Muslim community in Calgary. The association supports faith, education, cultural enrichment, community engagement and service.
